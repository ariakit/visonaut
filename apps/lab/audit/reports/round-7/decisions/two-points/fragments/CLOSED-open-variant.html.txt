    <span data-fact="counted"
      >The session code of the lab has the memory, and the page does not use it
      (<code>apps/lab/src/fixtures/hooks/review-model.ts</code>, lines 910 to 918, and

    >
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>What the new page does.</strong> A click on a row and a link with no variant keep the
    lab rule, which never opens an unchanged variant while the screenshot has a change. Up and Down
    get the memory of the app of today.
    <span data-fact="counted">In the app that is about 20 lines and 2 tests.</span>
    When the remembered variant is no longer in the run, the message goes to the visible status
    line, with the refusals of row 3 of <a href="#decision-D-WORK-01">D-WORK-01</a>: the finding
    of that row names this message too. <span data-findings="RULE-13"></span>
    The page forgets at a reload: nothing is stored, and nothing writes to D1.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>One sentence of the contract text is corrected.</strong>
    <span data-fact="counted"
      >Line 271 of the saved issue #1 text and line 82 of the review guide say that a first visit
      uses "its first variant". No source does that since your pull request #104, which gave the
      reason: "Item links could open an unchanged variant ahead of a mismatch".</span
    >
    The new text: a first visit opens the first variant that needs review.
    <span data-fact="measured"
      >In the lab scenario <code>one-browser</code>, 12 screenshots have 6 variants each, and only
      the 2 WebKit variants changed. The first variant is unchanged on each of the 12
      screenshots.</span
    >
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>What stays as it is.</strong>
    <span data-fact="measured"
