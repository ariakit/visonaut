<h4 id="ui-system-speed-report">A report to Ariakit UI</h4>

<p class="wide">
  Ariakit UI is the folder <code>packages/ariakit-ui</code> of the public repository
  ariakit/ariakit. The app copies its primitives. Of the four items above, two have a tested
  rewrite: item 1 (the hover glider of a nav) and item 3 (the sibling rule shape).
  <span data-fact="counted"
    >On the branch main of ariakit/ariakit, <code>styles/nav.ts</code> has the two hover rules of
    item 1 in lines 391 and 395, and <code>styles/ui.css</code> has the two custom variants of item
    3 in lines 1344 and 1535, each in the form as built (read on 2026-10-07).</span
  >
  So both rule forms are still upstream.
</p>

<p class="wide">
  The two rewrites do not have the same value for the service. Your answer to
  <a href="#decision-D-PERF-01">D-PERF-01</a> is the bar glider only, so the review page does not
  have the cost of item 1, and rewrite 1 is for the other users of Ariakit UI. Item 3 is different.
  <span data-fact="counted"
    >The app has its 3 rules today (<code>glider.ts</code>, lines 129 and 138, and
    <code>tabs.ariakit.react.tsx</code>, line 561).</span
  >
  Your answer to <a href="#decision-D-UI-02">D-UI-02</a> says that the app does not edit a copied
  file. So the review page keeps the cost of item 3 until Ariakit UI changes, and the test of
  <a href="#decision-D-PERF-02">D-PERF-02</a> only shows it.
  <span data-fact="measured"
    >In the lab with each folder open, 8 of the first 60 arrow keys recalculate 2,435 of 2,589
    elements, and 85 with rewrite 3. Nobody measured it in the app.</span
  >
</p>

<p class="wide">
  On 2026-10-07 you wrote: "I authorize the implementation, the GitHub writes, the two writes to
  production D1, and the package publication and the change in ariakit/ariakit." The plan that you
  answered had the line "Send the two tested rewrites to Ariakit UI early", and it listed the
  GitHub writes as "the branch, the issues, the pull requests". The record reads "the change in
  ariakit/ariakit" as the one pull request that updates the CLI and the capture adapter in Ariakit.
  Your words do not name a report about Ariakit UI, and they do not say its form. A report is a
  public text in a repository that is not the service. So the audit does not write it without your
  answer.
</p>
