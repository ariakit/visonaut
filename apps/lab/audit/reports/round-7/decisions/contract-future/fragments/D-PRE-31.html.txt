<h3 id="design-lab-contract">The contract and the settled design</h3>

<p>
  The contract is <code>docs/current-contract.md</code> on the branch main. A person or an agent
  reads it before a change, and its line 5 says: "Only an explicit approved change supersedes a
  requirement." Some of its rules are about the layout of the review page. The design that you
  settled in the lab breaks some of them.
  <span data-fact="counted"
    >The list of contract changes of revision r7 names none of these rules. The table above names
    one: the zoom levels.</span
  >
  So the first pull request that builds a page of the lab breaks the contract, and a reviewer or an
  agent that follows the contract must stop it or ask you.
</p>

<p>
  The table has the 5 rules that this revision verified. It is not complete: nobody compared each
  UI rule of the contract with each page of the lab.
  <span data-fact="counted"
    >The contract has its UI rules in three places: the rows U02 to U06 of its table (lines 143 to
    147), the section "Later approved review changes" (lines 192 and 194), and the section "Review
    UI and keyboard behavior" of the saved issue #1 text (lines 245 to 280).</span
  >
  Line 7 of the contract keeps each rule of the saved text in force until an approved rule replaces
  it. The decision <code>D-PRE-21</code> asks if that stays.
</p>

<p>
  Two of your answers of 2026-10-07 make this a question. The lab goes to a branch, and the
  contract is on the branch main, so a rule that names the lab names files that main does not have.
  And the contract is updated first, so it must agree with the design before the first page is
  built. The second option of the decision adds this row to the contract:
</p>

<pre><code class="language-text">
  Selected rule
    The settled design of the lab (30 lab decisions, lab record r3)

  Earlier promise or source rule
    U02 (line 143). Line 192, which replaced the U03 tabs. Line 194.
    Saved requirements line 262 (the thumbnail rule) and line 264 (the zoom levels).
    Each other rule about the layout, the place of a control, or the look,
    where the design differs.

  Exact replacement and retained scope
    The pages follow the settled design of the lab at one tag.
    Where an earlier rule about the layout, the place of a control, or the look
    differs, the design replaces it.

    Verified differences:
    1. A variant stepper and a row of marks that are links, with a cover on request,
       in place of variant links with a bar glider.
    2. Approve, Reject, and Undo in a floating bar at the bottom of the stage,
       in place of the header of the main panel.
    3. The list shows the screenshots with a change, and "Unchanged" is an entry
       of the status select, in place of the group Accepted.
    4. The picture of a list row shows the first variant that needs review,
       in place of the first declared variant.
    5. Zoom keeps fit, 100%, and 200%, and adds 50%, 400%, and 800%.

    Not changed: the keys, what one decision covers, the loaded images before a decision,
    the save and Undo rules, the privacy rules, the red mask, and Chrome Desktop.
</code></pre>
