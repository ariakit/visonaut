The scheduled `2026-09-25T00Z` production backup started at 00:01:45.753 UTC and reached `complete` at 00:13:24.591 UTC. It followed the 12:00 UTC completed slot from the previous day. The database row reports zero failures, seven required groups, **2,055 objects**, and **128,032,891 object bytes**. This is the first production backup after Ariakit PR #7635 submitted its 1,058-variant visual run.

The slot started 1 minute 45.753 seconds after its UTC boundary and completed 11 minutes 38.838 seconds after creation. At this completion, the previous completed snapshot's creation time was about 12 hours 12 minutes 50 seconds old. This measured interval is within the 24-hour recovery-point target for this one observed cycle. It does not establish sustained scheduling reliability.
